Red Card Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Red Card is a free app that moves betting and casino text messages to Junk on your iPhone. In short: no personal data is collected.

Your messages

Messages are checked only on your iPhone, through Apple's SMS filtering system. The filter has no internet access and, under Apple's rules, cannot store anything. Your messages never leave your phone; nobody, including the developer, can see them.

Your own rules

The words, numbers and strictness you set are stored only on your phone and are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Rule updates

To catch new betting tricks, the app may download a public rules file at most once a day. This is a download only: nothing about you or your messages is sent. As with any web request, the server hosting the file can technically see your IP address; Red Card does not collect or use it.

Tracking, ads, accounts

Red Card has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no in-app purchases.
