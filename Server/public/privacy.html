Mesajların

Mesajlar yalnızca iPhone'unda, Apple'ın SMS filtreleme altyapısıyla kontrol edilir. Filtrenin internet erişimi yoktur ve Apple'ın kuralları gereği hiçbir şey kaydedemez. Sen bir mesajı bildirmedikçe mesajların telefonundan çıkmaz.

Bir mesajı bildirmek tamamen isteğe bağlıdır.

Red Card Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Red Card is a free app that moves betting and casino text messages to Spam on your iPhone. In short: no personal data is collected. Only the text of messages you choose to report is received.

Your messages

Messages are checked only on your iPhone, through Apple's SMS filtering system. The filter has no internet access and, under Apple's rules, cannot store anything. Unless you report a message, your messages never leave your phone.

Messages you report

If you choose to report a missed betting message from the Messages app, only the following is sent to Red Card's server: the message text, the kind of sender (for example “mobile number” or “company name”), the day it arrived and the score Red Card gave it. The sender's number, your number, your name, device identifiers and your IP address are not stored. Reports are used only to teach the filter new betting messages and are never shared or sold. Reporting a message is entirely optional.

Your own rules

The words, numbers and strictness you set are stored only on your phone and are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Rule updates

To catch new betting tricks, the app may download a public rules file at most once a day. This is a download only: nothing about you or your messages is sent. As with any web request, the server hosting the file can technically see your IP address; Red Card does not collect or use it, and the server keeps no access logs.

Tracking, ads, accounts

Red Card has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no in-app purchases.
